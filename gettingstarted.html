Students receive a statement monthly or quarterly. To reconcile it, they:

  • Compare their statement to what's online at schoolsavings.com
  • Add any deposits made at the bank
  • Delete any withdrawals
  • Add interest earned
  • Confirm the balances match

Overview

School Savings is a social venture between a local financial institution, community schools and their families. The bank or credit union provides free savings or checking accounts plus student incentives. The school's PTA/PTO group helps coordinate the school banking process while volunteers accept deposits at school. School Savings provides the online deposit software and electronically credits the students' accounts through the Federal Reserve Bank eliminating manual posting and reconciliation.
