Our Mission is…

To inspire a new generation of savers by equipping financial institution partners with tools to build community relationships and promote financial literacy through school-based banking.

The School

  • Confirms participation to bank or credit union partner
  • Invites volunteers or staff members to coordinate program
  • Provides access to an Internet-ready computer once weekly before class or during lunch.

Students receive a statement monthly or quarterly. To reconcile it, they:

  • Compare their statement to what's online at schoolsavings.com
  • Add any deposits made at the bank
  • Delete any withdrawals
  • Add interest earned
  • Confirm the balances match

Overview

School Savings is a cooperative venture between a local financial institution and it's community schools. The financial institution provides the free saving account and student incentives while the school's PTA/PTO group manages the school banking process. School Savings provides the online deposit software and electronically credits the students' accounts through the Federal Reserve Bank eliminating manual posting and reconciliation.

Getting Started For Banks and Credit Unions

Select a local school and notify School Savings of your intent to implement at support@schoolsavings.com. All School Savings accounts are at the sponsor institution.

Send Account Enrollment Packets home with students. Sponsors may offer saving and or checking accounts. Accounts may be in the name of the student or be a more traditional custodial account.

Ask the PTA/PTO President to select Volunteers to collect student deposits and deliver them to a participating bank branch. Bankers may also collect deposits at school. Request Volunteer training from School Savings.

Request and sign a School Savings Agreement. Receive School Savings login credentials. 888-787-7728 | CONTACT: support@schoolsavings.com

Offering small savings incentives and having contests makes saving fun! Advertise your First Deposit Day CONTEST to students, parents and the media to FIRE-UP participation.
